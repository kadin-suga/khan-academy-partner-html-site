July 18, 2023

Khan Academy × Walmart

Financial literacy, free to learn.

On July 18, 2023, Khan Academy and Walmart announced a partnership in which Walmart became the founding sponsor of Khan Academy’s free financial literacy course for Walmart associates, customers, and the wider community.
